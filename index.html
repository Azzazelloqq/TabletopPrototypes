Tabletop minigame prototypes

Playable prototypes of three in-game board games, played against a computer opponent.

  • Topple — stack dice on your towers or throw them at the rival's.
  • Rat Chute — shove sacks through chutes; gold or rats fall out the far end.
  • Tumbledown — dice soldiers tumble across the board; the face that rolls up is the number they strike with.
  • Put — three cards, best of three tricks, and a bluff call.
